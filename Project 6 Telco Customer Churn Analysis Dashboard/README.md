# Telco Customer Churn Analysis
## Overview
A multi-page Power BI project analyzing customer churn, customer behaviour, revenue and churn drivers.
## Tools Used
- Excel
- Python (Pandas, Jupyter Notebook)
- SQL
- Power BI (Power Query, DAX)
## Dashboard Pages
- Overview - Key business KPIs and overall churn
- Customers - Customer demographics and behaviour
- Revenue Analysis - Revenue and charge analysis
- Churn Drivers - Services, tenure, number of services and churn reasons
## Key Insights
- Customer churn varies significantly across tenure, services, contracts and customer segments.
- Shorter tenure customers show higher churn, highlighting the importance of early customer retention.
- Customers with greater service engagement generally show lower churn.
- Revenue and charge patterns reveal important differences across customer groups and payment methods.
- Pricing, service experience, and competitor related factors are among the major reasons customers leave.
